Thank you for testing. Five minutes to set up, then you play. Nothing to sign up for and no code to type.

1. UNZIP EVERYTHING FIRST
   Mac: double-click the zip. Windows: right-click the zip, then Extract All...
   Don't start the game from inside the zip: it needs the files beside it.

2. OPEN THE GAME
   Mac: open {product}.app. The first time, macOS says it can't check the app (it isn't from
   the App Store). Open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down, click Open Anyway,
   then open the app again. Keep the app in its folder.
   Windows: open {product}.exe. If Windows says it protected your PC, click More info, then
   Run anyway. Keep the {product}_Data folder beside the .exe.

3. CHOOSE WHETHER TO SEND
   Play and send: when you finish, what happened in the game goes to the developer as a 3D
   replay, and may be used to train Karen. Play without sending: nothing leaves your computer.

4. PLAY UP TO THREE SHIFTS
   A short walkthrough shows the first steps. Something broke or confused you? Press
   Shift+F7 and type a line about it.

5. FINISH, ANSWER, DONE
   A few optional questions follow, and the game sends your session by itself. Offline, it
   sends it the next time you open the game.

YOUR DATA
