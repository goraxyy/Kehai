Thank you for testing. Five minutes to set up, then you play. Nothing to sign up for and no code to type.

I'm on a MacI'm on Windows
1

Unzip everything first

Mac: double-click the zip. Windows: right-click the zip, then Extract All….

Don't start the game from inside the zip: it needs the files beside it.

On a Mac

2

Open {product}.app

The first time, macOS says it can't check the app. That's expected: it isn't from the App Store.

Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down, click Open Anyway, then open the app again.

Keep the app in its folder: the camera helper for the optional blinking sits beside it.

On Windows

2

Open {product}.exe

Windows may say it protected your PC. That's expected for a new game.

Click More info, then Run anyway.

Keep the {product}_Data folder beside the .exe.

In the game

3

Choose whether to send

Play and send: when you finish, what happened in the game goes to the developer as a 3D replay, and may be used to train Karen. Play without sending: nothing leaves your computer. You can play either way.

4

Play up to three shifts

A short walkthrough shows the first steps. Something broke or confused you? Press Shift + F7 and type a line about it.

5

Finish, answer, done

Quit, or finish after shift three. A few optional questions follow, and the game sends your session by itself. If you're offline, it sends it the next time you open the game.

Your data

Recorded: where you go in the game, what you press, what Karen does, and how smoothly it runs. Never your camera, microphone or screen.

It's kept for six months. To have it deleted sooner, email {email} with the ID the game shows on its last screen.
