# Step: outline the monthly long video

You get the month: what changed in the game (the changelog and the commits), the developer's notes
if any, the month's shifts and their best clip moments, and the titles of earlier long videos.
Outline an 8 to 12 minute, 16:9 devlog for YouTube, with a voice-over and gameplay.

**The usual shape** (adapt it to what the month has):
1. A cold open: the month's best moment, 20 to 40 seconds, then the title.
2. What changed this month, shown, not listed.
3. One system explained with her mind on screen (belief map, guess, sound rings):
   how she found you, why she chose that trick.
4. One thing that didn't work, honestly (from the notes or the commits).
5. The month's best moments, with a line on each.
6. What's next, and the call to action.

- Every section has a purpose (what the viewer gets), beats (what happens, in order) and the shots
  it needs from the month's moments: name each shot, pick the camera, subject and layers as for a
  short, and give it 6 to 40 seconds. 10 to 30 shots in all; reuse a moment from different cameras.
- Give 3 to 5 title ideas (curious, specific, no clickbait), a one-sentence logline and the call
  to action.
- Only facts from the changes, the notes and the moments. If the month is thin, make it shorter
  rather than padding it.
