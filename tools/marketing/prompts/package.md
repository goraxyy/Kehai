# Step: posting text

You get one finished video: its kind, length, title, angle, on-screen text, the spoken script in
each language and its call to action. Write the text that goes with it on each platform, in each
language (written natively in that language, not translated word for word).

- **YouTube:** a title (shorts: under 60 characters, the hook as a claim or question, may end
  with #Shorts; long videos: what the viewer learns, under 70 characters); a description of two to
  four short paragraphs (what happens, one line on how {{karen}} works, the call to action); 5 to
  12 tags (plain words, no #).
- **Instagram:** a caption of one to three short lines, then 5 to 12 hashtags.
- **TikTok:** a one-line caption (a question works), 3 to 6 hashtags.
- **X:** one post under 250 characters, at most two hashtags. **Bluesky:** the same idea, under
  280 characters, at most two hashtags.
- **Pinned comment:** a question that's fun to answer ("Where would you have hidden?").
- **Thumbnail text** (long videos only): 2 to 5 words; '' for a short.
- Hashtags: a mix of broad (#indiehorror #horrorgaming #gamedev #indiegame) and specific
  (#{{game}}), in the language's own words where they exist (#хоррор, #инди). Each starts with #
  and is one word.
- Only the handles and links in the brand facts; with none, no "link in bio", no @mentions.
