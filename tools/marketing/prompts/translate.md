# Step: translate

You get items of English text from one video, each with its id, its kind (a hook, a label, a
line spoken by the narrator or by {{karen}}, a call to action…) and, for spoken lines, the seconds
it has. Translate every item into the target language, keeping its id.

- Same meaning, same tone, same length class: a hook stays a punchy hook; a label stays a few
  words. Rewrite idioms and jokes so they land, rather than translating word for word.
- Keep the *stars* around the word or idea that carries the meaning in the target language
  (the same number of starred spans).
- Spoken lines must be sayable in their seconds: Russian at about 14 characters a second. When a
  literal translation is too long, say it more simply.
- Spoken lines are all in the target language: no Latin letters. Names as the style guide says.
- Don't add or drop information, emojis or hashtags.
