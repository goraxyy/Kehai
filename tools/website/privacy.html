Privacy

What's recorded, and for how long.

Short version: only what happens inside the game, only if you agree, kept for six months, deleted sooner if you ask.

In the playtest

When you first open the game it asks: Play and send or Play without sending. You can play either way.

  • If you choose to send: what happens in the game goes to the developer as a session. It includes where you go and look, what you press, what Karen does, how smoothly the game runs, your computer's type, any bug notes, and your answers to the optional questions at the end (with a nickname, if you give one).
  • Never recorded: your camera, microphone or screen. The optional webcam blinking reads your camera on your computer only: no picture from it ever leaves.
  • Why: the developer watches sessions as 3D replays to fix what was confusing or broken. Sessions may also be used to train Karen, the game's AI.
  • If you choose not to send: nothing leaves your computer. No session, no answers, no usage counts.

The game gives each install a random ID, so sessions from one computer can be grouped. It isn't your name, an account or a login.

How long

Six months from when a session arrives, then it's deleted: the session, its rows, and its copies in any training set made from it. Downloading a session for analysis doesn't restart that clock.

Deleting your data sooner

Email tokenlimitlabs@gmail.com with the ID the game shows on its last screen. Everything from that ID is deleted, and later sends from it are refused.

On this website

  • Downloads are counted per day and per Mac or Windows, nothing more.
  • Page visits are counted by Cloudflare Web Analytics, which uses no cookies.
  • The email sign-up keeps your address only to send the playtest email, until you unsubscribe. Reply to any email to unsubscribe.
