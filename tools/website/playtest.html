Playtest

Play a night shift.

Kehai is a horror game about a night shift in a convenience store, and the store's AI, Karen, who works out where you are. This build is for playtesters: play up to three shifts.

Download

The first time you open it

Unzip it first

Mac: double-click the zip. Windows: right-click it, then Extract All…. Don't open the game from inside the zip. The folder has a START HERE guide too.

On a Mac

macOS says it can't check the app, because it isn't from the App Store. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down, click Open Anyway, then open the app again.

On Windows

If Windows says it protected your PC, click More info, then Run anyway.

What gets recorded

In the game you choose Play and send or Play without sending. If you send, the developer gets what happened in the game: where you went, what you pressed, what Karen did, and how smoothly it ran, as a 3D replay. It may be used to train Karen. Never your camera, microphone or screen. More on privacy.
