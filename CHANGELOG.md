# Changelog

- **2026-09-29** — Renamed: the game is now **Kehai** (気配) and its antagonist **Aiko** (愛子); they were Karoshi and Karen. The first launch copies the old save data and settings across (`KehaiMigration.cs`).
- **2026-10-02** — A main menu: Continue picks the career up at its next shift (Aiko's ledger is the save), New career wipes it, and the Esc menu can leave a shift for it. The first macOS build, from `KehaiBuild.MacOS`.
- **2026-10-04** — A Windows build (64-bit), from `KehaiBuild.Windows`; the editor opens the store when it starts on an empty scene.
- **2026-10-04** — Playtest builds (`-playtest <round>`): tester code, consent, the whole session recorded from launch (3D replays between shifts too), bug notes, end-of-session questions, packed for sending. See `PLAYTEST.md`.
- **2026-10-06** — Real products on the shelves: the 80 catalogue products as low-poly models with their own labels (`Kehai/Products/1. Import Product Models`), stocked by a supermarket planogram (eye level, kids' level, heavy low, brand blocks, end caps, cross-merchandising) in blocks that fill each slot. Hanging aisle and department signs. Customers shop from a list in store order and ask for products by name, by aisle or by department, or when a shelf is empty. See `STORE_CATALOG.md` §7, §9 and §11.
- **2026-10-07** — Full shelves: every board cut into 0.5 m squares, each filled with real slots for what it sells (2×2 small packs, 2×1 long ones, drinks by footprint), 16,500 items in all, shown 1.5× real size so labels read. Every part of the shop is a numbered aisle (1 by the door, 13 at the tills) under a black hanging sign; a lamp over every ceiling light, dark when its light is off; each room's lights kept in their room. The main menu's first line no longer stays lit under the cursor. Docs sorted into `docs/`, with `docs/design/MERCHANDISING.md` on how the shelves are stocked.
- **2026-10-07** — Fixed the same day: the rooms' lights lit only people and stock, never the rooms (URP drops rendering layers that have no name, and the rooms were on 8–12; they're on 1–5 now). Items stocked in the editor stood 0.2 m above their boards. Mochi Bites and the hand sanitiser were on no shelf, because their aisles lack the boards their layouts use; every product now gets at least one facing. Aiko's design is `docs/design/Aiko.md`: the move had put her name in capitals, and it is never shouted.
- **2026-10-07** — The GPU Resident Drawer draws the shop, with GPU occlusion culling: the camera's render cost over four fixed views went from 18.9 to 4.5 ms. The plan for far more stock and a maze without an end is in `docs/design/IDEAS.md` (Scaling): shelves as data next, then chunks and streaming.
- **2026-10-07** — Shelves as data (#35): a shelf slot is a record, the stock on it is drawn with instanced calls (in the editor too, with nothing baked), and an item is a GameObject only once it leaves a shelf. The store's scene went from 92 MB to 6.2 MB. `Kehai/Store/Migrate to Data Shelves` clears out a scene from before, and now finds the till counter's old slot too.
- **2026-10-07** — The shelf stock is drawn as bare render objects that the GPU Resident Drawer takes up (#38–#40): 2.2 to 2.4 ms over the four test views, where instanced calls cost 5 to 7. Checked in Play: aiming at shelves, taking and putting back, Aiko's sweep, and shoppers.
- **2026-10-07** — An endless maze (#37), in a scene of its own (`Kehai/Endless/Open an Endless Maze`): 25 m chunks worked out from a seed, braided so it's mostly loops, built around the player a chunk a frame and taken down again, keeping what changed on their shelves. Each chunk sells one section. A NavMesh follows the player.
