## 1. How to use this plan

**Tick as you go.** Change `- [ ]` to `- [x]` in the same commit as the work, and update the
progress table at the top.
- A task's box is ticked when its *done when* holds and its tests pass.
- The smaller boxes under a task are its steps: tick them as they land, so a half-done task shows
  what's left.

## 4. Each trick, end to end

Each block: what you see · what she does · your chore · how she learns · the tasks.

### Fog (#2)
- **You see:** a hiss from the fridges, then white fog rolling out of every fridge in the room until it fills the room. Inside it you can see about 2 m.
- **She:** picks the room she thinks you're in, if it has fridges. Inside the fog *her* sight stops at 2 m too, and so does a camera's and Kenji's. Her hearing is unchanged.
- **Chore:** the fridge control box in the backstreet, next to the breaker box. Hold E for 3 s; the fog thins over about 3 s. Its light blinks while the fog is on.
- **Learns:** which room pays (placement memory, [§5](#5-how-karen-learns-after-030)), and how fast you reset it.
- **Tasks:** C2.1, B2.1, C6.1, B6.1.

> **Distance fog,** a new term. *What:* the renderer fades everything beyond a set distance into a
> colour. *In Kehai:* while your camera is inside a fogged room, the fade ends at the visibility
> (2 m), and fog particles at the room's doorways and fridges make it look thick from outside too.
> *Elsewhere:* the "render distance" fog in Minecraft, or a car game's fog setting.

### Camera bolt-on (#3)
- **You see:** a drill sound, then a small camera standing in a shelf slot like a product, pointing up an aisle. While it sees you it beeps once a second and its red light goes solid.
- **She:** picks a slot and an angle that cover where you usually hide or walk ([§5](#5-how-karen-learns-after-030)), walks to the bay and fits it. Every sighting reaches her wherever she is, as camera sightings do today.
- **Chore:** unplug it (hold E for 4 s), or stay out of its view. Unplugging tells her someone's there, as today.
- **Learns:** slot and angle, from how long each camera saw you last time.
- **Tasks:** C3.5, B3.2, C6.1, B6.1.

### Shelf sweep (#4)
- **You see:** shelves rattling, then 5–15 products dropping onto the floor. The bay lights up for restocking, and the task list shows "Restock the shelves · Aisle 4".
- **She:** stands right in front of the bay, waits until she hears nobody near, then knocks the products off.
- **Chore:** restock the bay. The products on the floor are loud to walk over.
- **Learns:** which bays pay: far enough from you to go unseen, and on your way.
- **Tasks:** C3.1.

### Spill (#5)
- **You see:** a bucket clank, then the same dirt shoppers leave. "Mop up spills" unticks.
- **She:** chooses a spot on the way she thinks you'll take, walks there, and drops it at her feet.
- **Chore:** mop it, or walk through and leave footprints.
- **Learns:** which spots you walk through.
- **Tasks:** C3.2.

### Bin tamper (#6)
- **You see:** a lid clattering; "Empty the trash" unticks.
- **She:** must be at the bin (rule 9), and only uses bins inside her area.
- **Chore:** bag it, take it out back.
- **Tasks:** C3.3.

### Tool theft (#8)
- **You see:** a rattle in the WC, then the WC mop gone. The task list says "Return the WC mop". Its rack says "She moved it. Find it." and won't recall it.
- **She:** walks to the WC rack, takes the mop (it's in her hand), drops it somewhere and leaves. She never touches the staff room mop.
- **Chore:** find it and carry it back, or put it on its rack.
- **Combo:** she can wait near where she left it (Ambush; [§5](#5-how-karen-learns-after-030)).
- **Learns:** where to drop it.
- **Tasks:** C3.4, B3.1.

### The overtime (#9)
- **You see:** a buzz from the staff room. The task list's timer freezes and says "Clock stopped". A job appears: "Restart the time clock".
- **She:** walks to the time clock in the shift's last 2 minutes and stops it. Customers keep coming while it's stopped.
- **Chore:** hold E at the time clock for 3 s.
- **Replaces** today's version, which refused your clock-out and added 120 s.
- **Tasks:** C2.4, B2.4.

### The PA box (#11)
- **You see:** a box with a switch in the staff room: "Switch the PA off" / "on". The breaker box keeps its three light switches only.
- **She:** can't play anything through the PA while it's off: Kenji's welcome, Back off's hold music, the Director's PA noises. Her thought log says so. She can't switch it back on.
- **Tasks:** B2.2, C2.2.

### Phantom chime (#16)
- **You see:** the front doors near where she thinks you are slide open and shut, chiming, for 20 s, with nobody there.
- **She:** her own door noises don't fool her own ears.
- **Chore:** check them, or don't.
- **Tasks:** C2.3, B2.3.

### Block door (#18)
- **You see:** a thud. A box sits on a floor frame right behind a door, on the side away from you.
  - From your side, the door rattles and says "Blocked from the other side".
  - From the box's side: "Pick up the box to unblock the door".
- **She:** picks the door from your routes, and the side away from where she expects you to come. She walks to that side to place it. There's no timer: the door stays shut until someone moves the box. The "can you still finish the shift" check (rule 6) stays.
- **Every door swings both ways.** The staff room's doors swing one way today (`StaffRoomModels.cs:434`).
- **Tasks:** C4.1, B4.1.

### The understudy (#20)
- **You see:** Kenji, the new hire, comes in through the front doors (the PA welcomes him, if it's on).
  - He keeps 10–20 m behind you.
  - While he can see you, he stands still and watches, and she knows where you are.
  - When he loses you, he follows.
- **Chore:** walk up to him and press E. Pick "Karen needs you on the tills" and he leaves by the front doors.
- **Once a shift.**
- **Tasks:** C4.2, B4.2.

## 5. How Karen learns, after 0.3.0

**Today she learns three things** (0.2.0):

| What | How | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Which trick | A score per trick: how much it scared you (panic change), fading over shifts | Blackout paid well last shift, so she leans on it |
| Which pairs | The combo room counts tricks started while another's still running | Blackout, then Ambush on the way to the breakers |
| Your habits | The Ledger: where you hide, your routes, what you answer | You always hide in Aisle 12 |

**0.3.0 changes five things:**

1. **More to choose from:** 17 choices instead of 9, across all tiers.

2. **Where to do each trick: placement memory** (new).
   > *What:* for each trick, a score per place: how well doing it *there* paid last time, fading
   > over shifts. She mostly picks the best place she knows, and sometimes tries a new one.
   > *In Kehai:* places come from her Ledger (your habits and routes), then the score sorts them.
   > *Elsewhere:* Google Maps preferring the route you actually take, or YouTube mixing a new
   > video in with the kinds you've liked.

   | Trick | A "place" is | Candidates from | Paid by |
   |---|---|---|---|
   | Camera | a slot and an angle (8 turns × 3 tilts) | slots that see your habit spots | seconds it saw you, and sightings that led to a check or a hunt |
   | Spill | a region | your predicted routes | you walked through it, or went round |
   | Shelf sweep | a bay | full bays on your way, 10–30 m off | time until you restocked it |
   | Block door | a door and a side | your routes' doors | how far you had to go round |
   | Tool theft | a drop region | the places you visit least, and Ambush spots | time until you found it |
   | Fog | a room | rooms with fridges | panic change |
   | Phantom chime | a pair of doors | the doors near you | whether you went to look |

   It lives in the Ledger, per player, is saved with it, and fades like everything else she
   learns (rule 7). Learning from *all* players comes with the learning plan in
   [PR #76](https://github.com/goraxyy/Kehai/pull/76): placement memory is built so that plan can pool it.

3. **Your answers count.** Every chore records what you did and how long it took:
   - `fog_reset`, `clock_restarted`, `mop_returned`, `box_moved`, `understudy_sent_away`, `pa_off`
   - today's `camera_unplugged`

   A trick you answer fast scares you less, so its score drops and she moves on. The shift report
   lists your answers.

4. **Combos through chores.** Ambush already waits on your way to the breaker box after a
   blackout. It now waits on your way to whatever her running trick sent you to (`FixAt`): the
   fridge box, the clock, the mop, the far side of a blocked door. The combo room then learns
   which pairs pay, for example Tool theft then Ambush.

5. **New eyes, and fog for everyone.** Cameras and Kenji report sightings to her belief, as today.
   Fog caps every eye inside it, hers included.

**Not changed:** the Director, the bandit's maths, the fairness gate, and rung F (built, not trained;
its action list just gets shorter).

## 7. Tests and metrics

**Primary metrics,** fixed before any run:
1. **Every live trick works end to end:** it fires, lands, shows its chore, and fixing the chore clears it. Pass or fail per trick (C5.5).
2. **Fairness violations: 0,** under rules 1, 2 and 4–9.
3. **No stuck shifts:** every test shift ends before its time-out.
4. **Setpoint error** against `main`, paired by seed.

**Secondary:**
- catches, catches in fog;
- how often each live trick is used;
- seconds to fix each chore;
- seconds each camera saw you;
- whether the place memory improves between a career's first and last shifts;
- Ambush catches after a chore.

**The ablation:**
- rungs C and E;
- three training players (efficient, skittish, reckless);
- three careers, shifts 1–10, so every trick unlocks;
- the cautious player is held out: reported on its own, never used to tune;
