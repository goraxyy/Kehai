  material, generated helper binary, Packages and ProjectSettings files stay out of git.

  application separately from source delivery, and regenerate scene/navigation maps using
  their tools after actual changes; never hand-edit generated maps.
- Preserve fair sensing: observed entry creates a last-seen search target, not continuing
  access to the player's actual hidden position. The owner superseded physical-touch range with a 3 m catch for witnessed entry into a
  still-occupied hiding place, including behind its door. Test leaving the hiding place after sight is lost.
- Apply one power rule to every automatic-door request, including Karen and phantom chimes.
  Reconcile overlapping global blackout and room-light effects; restoration must not leave
  doors or lights disabled, nor cancel an unrelated effect. Check navigation through stopped
  panels and access to the power repair so blackout cannot trap a shift permanently.
- Inspect ambient/sky contribution, baked or probe lighting, emission, exposure, fog and local
  lights together. Do not substitute a screen overlay for actual lighting changes. Check
  perception's darkness input against the rendered state rather than silently retuning Karen.
- Two-way manual opening must respect panel collisions. Reversal changes target from the
  current angle with no snapping; locks/physical blocks still prevent invalid movement.
- Helper work includes compile/setup instructions, lookup and packaging for supported targets,
  with consent and process cleanup preserved. No camera recording or activation in this plan.

## Additional scope: items 12–14

### Office camera removal

Inspect the actual reported object before removal. `_Game/Level/Scripts/StoreCameras.cs`
defines six feeds outside the office (cold room, stockroom, lobby, staff room, store front,

### Lost-sight pursuit

`KarenConfig.huntMemorySeconds` defaults to 6f. `Actions.cs` scores continuation against
that memory; `Primitives.cs` Hunt.HandOver also exits on an empty goal after 0.5 seconds,
recovery or outside belief. This is source behaviour, not a measured live chase duration.
Trace planner/tactic timeouts and all brain modes before proposing the complete change.
Use the owner's plan's proposed deadline policy; distinguish continuing pursuit from
searching a predicted area. Do not just replace 6 with 60 and claim the behaviour is done.

Later checks: uninterrupted loss for the full proposed interval, early empty destination,
new sighting near the deadline, repeated noises, witnessed hiding, blocked routes, outside
boundary, recovery and stun during pursuit. Decide explicitly that stun consumes elapsed
pursuit time, rather than secretly extending the pursuit. Log why the hunt/search ends.
The 60-second duration is included in the authorised implementation scope.

### Physical object hits and Karen stun

`_Game/AI/Scripts/Core/KarenBody.cs` creates a kinematic Rigidbody and a trigger capsule
for touch catches. `_Game/Items/Scripts/Item.cs` has collision audio, gated by the presence
of an audio clip; this must not become the gate for damage/stun. `PlayerEffects.Stun` is
for the player and does not implement Karen stun. Inspect `KarenBrain.OnTouched`, navigation,
blink dash, active tactics and attack execution before designing the shared stun state.

- Add appropriate physical item collision separately from the catch trigger. Keep her
  navigation body stable; do not turn her into an uncontrolled dynamic rigidbody. Check
  layer filtering and existing sight-ray exclusions so her own collider cannot blind her.
- Estimate impact from pre-contact relative closing velocity along the contact normal and
  bounded item mass. Clamp/validate inputs and duration. Prefer this controllable score to
  solver force; document exact units and thresholds once chosen. Detect moving loose items,
  reject carried/shelved/resting items and Karen's own spawned throws.
- Combine continuous collision handling or swept tests as needed for fast throws; deduplicate
  contacts across multiple colliders and frames. A duplicate callback cannot extend the stun.
  Define a bounded repeat-hit policy and a short rearm rule; no indefinite resting-contact stun.
- Centralise stun so movement, dash, attacks, hand interactions and touch catches all respect
  it. Define same-step hit/catch precedence: a valid stun impact must suppress a later catch
  in that simulation step, independent of callback order. Do not resurrect an already ended shift.
- On expiry, restore navigation and replan from current sensed information. Reset cleanly on
  new shift, restart, disable/despawn and scene reload. Pausing freezes the stun timer. Avoid
  re-enabling an attack or navigation component that was disabled for another reason.

Required later checks: different masses/speeds and glancing hits; stationary or carried items;
items with no audio clip; wall-occluded throws; fast small projectiles and low frame rates;
multiple colliders, simultaneous objects and bounces; floor clutter and collision pileups;
hits while chasing, searching, dashing, using a door or already stunned; player touching her
on the impact step and during stun; pause/resume; expiry and repeated recovery; restart and
scene unload. Verify actual physical blocking/deflection as well as the stun timer. Include
frame-time/physics stability and a playable escape-and-reacquisition check. No tests run yet.
