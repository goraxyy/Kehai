# Changelog

- **2026-11-28 — 0.5.2 branch, not released.** Frame rate and the back-room doors.
  - **The mini store ran at about 10 frames a second; most of it was the security cameras.** All six feeds re-drew the whole store every frame wherever you stood (47 of 73 ms). They now draw only while the office screens are in view, two a frame in turn (each screen about 20 times a second at 60 fps), without shadows or post-processing. The store side keeps its rule (near the screens, one feed at a time) and gains the same in-view test. Measured in the editor: the main thread went from about 80 ms to about 15–20 ms.
  - **Big screens draw fewer pixels and scale up with FSR.** A Retina Mac's 2880 × 1688 is 2.3 times 1080p, and the frame's GPU time grows with it. High now draws at most 2.6 million pixels (about 1440p), Medium 1.9 and Low 1.3; smaller screens are unchanged.
  - **No light down the sides of the stockroom and cold room doors.** Those doors are a 1.84 m leaf in a 2 m opening (the model's 2 m panel is switched off). The mini store's frames now fit the visible leaf, and the store gets a trim of the leaf's own finish round each such leaf (visual only).

- **2026-11-28 — 0.5.2 branch, not released.** Codex's roadside art, taken into the game (the owner: "do the fixes yourself").
  - **The art's game side is the game's own code now:** the mini store attaches the art if it's in the project (`MiniStoreRoadside`), product looks can be swapped by an art layer (`ProductLook.SetVisualLayer`), and `Kehai → Art → Import roadside exchange` turns the Blender exporter's output into the art.   - **The art fogs** like the rest of the store (the replays turn fog on).
  - **The third lobby recycling bin moves out of automatic doors 3 and 4.** Halving the store's spots had put it 11 cm off the wall, inside the doorway's width. The art's lobby moves the other two bins as well, only when the art is there. A new check: nothing stands in front of an automatic door.

- **2026-11-25 — 0.5.2 branch, not released.** How Karen finds you when you hide (Phase 9).
  - **She checks hiding places:** when something tells her you might be there (a noise from it, a customer's word, where you usually hide), she may walk up to a fridge or locker, open it and look, or kneel at a stockroom rack and look under it. If you're inside, and under a rack within 1.5 m of her, you're caught. She's never certain to check, and leaves a place she just checked alone for a while.
  - **Who saw you hide:** a store camera while she watches the screens, or a shopper she then asks, can tell her where you went in. A torch left on gives away its light, not you.
  - **Decoys:** anything you throw lands with a noise she goes to. Throwing no longer gives you away: your hand makes no "something dropped" noise, and the item can't thud against you on the way out.
  - **Doors:** a door she sees still swinging sends her through it. Wait until she can't see it, or shut it behind you (a shut door gives her only its swing).
  - **The test players** know both: they throw a product away from where they'll hide before going there, and wait at a door she can see.

- **2026-11-17 — 0.5.1 branch, not released.** The owner's fourteen 0.5.0 playtest fixes.
  - **Karen's eyes:** a short shelf no longer slows her down if she can see your eyes over it. Inside a fridge cabinet, locker, toilet stall, changing cabin or the empty WC cupboard, or crouched under a stockroom rack, you're hidden even with the door open. Shoppers can't see you there either.
  - **Seen hiding:** if she sees you go in, she runs to where you went in and catches you within 3 m of that spot if you're still there. Unseen, you stay hidden; slip out and her memory is useless.
  - **Light:** stronger pools under the lamps and dark between them; the parking lot is dark; a blackout is pitch black (in 0.5.0 fog lit it white); power back restores the look.
  - **Names** float over the clocks, the time clock, the coffee machine, the PA box and microphone, the breakers and the other fixtures you can use. The PA microphone, stuck 3 m up the wall, now stands on its own small table.
  - **Doors:** the cold-room and stockroom doors open on one press (one press reached them twice and shut them again). Room doors open away from you from either side. In a blackout the automatic doors are dead (no chime, nobody opens them), and you can slide one open by hand.
  - **Burnout:** holding Shift while standing still no longer drains you at the sprint rate.
  - **The hunt:** after she loses sight of you she keeps hunting for up to 60 s from her last real sighting, searching where she heard you, where she guesses you went and where she last saw you, instead of giving up after about 6 s. Noises steer her but don't extend it.
  - **Throwing things at her:** a loose item that hits her hard enough stuns her (up to 3 s): she stops moving, can't catch you, then carries on. Grazes don't count; the same throw can't stun twice.
  - **Fog** is neutral grey, not blue. **The settings footer** has four equal buttons and says "Restart". **The cold room's** two islands are closer together. **The office camera** prop that never worked is gone. **The webcam helper** builds itself on a Mac and ships inside Mac builds.
