## Proposed defaults to review

- Names appear above nearby usable objects, face the player, and disappear behind walls;
  the existing E/action prompt remains separate. They should stay legible without lighting the room.
- During blackout, automatic panels stop where they are; all powered requests are ignored.
  On restoration, sensors are reconciled before motion resumes. This avoids inventing powered
  closing during a power cut. Exact blackout panel behaviour is a proposal, not a confirmed choice.
- “Open both ways” applies to manual door swings. Automatic sliding doors keep sliding, with
  approach sensors on both sides. Check fridge/locker geometry for two-way clearance rather
  than silently retaining a one-way exception.
- The two islands' final spacing is chosen in the actual scene, after checking door and walking
  clearance; no unmeasured coordinates are prescribed here.

## Pursuit and stun recommendations

**Pursuit:** the source default is 6 seconds of hunt memory, with earlier exits for an empty
predicted destination, recovery, or belief that you went outside. A live override could differ.
There is room for a minute-long pursuit/search, but changing the number alone will not prevent
those early exits. Proposed: an overall 60-second window since the last confirmed sighting,
with empty destinations leading into search; a new sighting refreshes it. Noises steer search
but do not grant hidden position tracking or endlessly refresh this deadline. Preserve the
outside boundary and explicitly review recovery pacing before changing it.

**Stun:** use impact strength from relative closing speed and bounded object mass, not raw
world speed or physics force (which can depend on the simulation step). A light graze gives
little or no stun; a fast, heavier thrown object gives more. Set a minimum impact threshold
and a maximum duration; exact thresholds/durations wait for playtesting. Repeated contacts
from the same collision must not stack, and resting or held objects must not create a stun loop.
This needs collision handling as well as an AI state change. The implementation must include
focused physics and recovery checks; “no bugs” cannot be guaranteed by a plan.
