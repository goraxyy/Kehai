## 1. The problem

Against the simulated players, 0.2.0 catches you in almost every shift:

| Player | Catches per shift, `main` → 0.2.0 | Shift length (s) | Finished the shift |
|---|---|---|---|
| efficient | 0.42 → 1.00 | 305 → 103 | 31% → 0% |
| skittish | 0.33 → 0.97 | 330 → 136 | 3% → 0% |
| reckless | 0.74 → 1.00 | 232 → 82 | 11% → 0% |
| cautious (held out) | 0.39 → 0.97 | 296 → 94 | 6% → 3% |

**Why it matters:**
- **It breaks the game's first idea.** [Karen.md](../../agents/karen/Karen.md#0-tldr) says she "almost never wins
  by catching you": she wins by making the shift take longer than you have energy for. Today a
  shift ends in a catch about 100 s in.
- **It hides the rest of 0.2.0.** She almost never loses you, so Hunt on her guesses, Cut and the
  combo room hardly run, and the main hunt metric (the gap 5 s after she loses you) can't be
  measured: 0 pairs.

## 2. Why she catches you

Each catch is logged with what she was doing. In the ablation's 0.2.0 run:

| What she was doing | Catches | Share |
|---|---|---|
| **Hunting**, at a run (7.5 m/s) | 82 | 58% |
| **Checking a spot**, at a hurry (4.2 m/s) | 56 | 39% |
| No plan running | 4 | 3% |

**Four causes:**
1. **She sees you sooner.** First detection comes about 9 s into a shift, against 32–61 s on
   `main`, and 0.8 times a shift from beyond 18 m. That's the wider, longer cone, working as
   designed.
2. **She out-runs you.** A Hunt sprints at 7.5 m/s against your 6.5. In a shift that ends in a
   catch she hunts for only about 2 s in all, so most hunts are won almost as soon as they start.
3. **Check hurries into you.** Check always hurries, at 4.2 m/s, a little faster than your 4.0 m/s
   walk, and **a touch kills in every action except Lure**. So a Check that reaches you is a catch.
4. **The bots notice her less than a person would.** They can't hear her steps (players can't
   either, since 0.2.0), and they don't see her spotlight, which a person can: it lights the floor
   up to 25 m ahead of her. A third of the catches (32%) came where the bot hadn't seen her in the
   5 s before.

**Not the cause:** the blink dash (0.08 close-ins a shift), Throw (5 hits in 144 shifts) and Cut
(it never fired).

**Not known yet**, because nothing measures it: how far away she is when a Hunt starts, how long a
Hunt lasts before the catch, and seconds from first sight to her moving at you (the build plan's
primary metric 1).

**Also found:** each catch's log line says "chasing False", even during a Hunt. `Kill()` stops the
plan, which clears the flag, before it writes the line. It's only the log; her behaviour is right.

## 3. Your decisions

**12 November answers:** Q3 is yes (touch outside Hunt stays lethal), Q4 is no (do not
reduce sprint below 7.5 m/s). Q2 and Q5 remain open while their meaning is explained.
Q1's target was already accepted in the 0.4.0 plan. The allowed sweep below reflects these
answers; L1 and L3 are excluded, not awaiting implementation.

| # | Question | My recommendation |
|---|---|---|
| **Q1** | **What catch rate is the target,** for the training players? | **A third of shifts or fewer: 0.20–0.35 catches a shift.** Fewer than `main` (0.45–0.54), because the design says catches should be rare; not near 0, so she stays dangerous. The playtest then checks it with people |
| **Q2** | **Keep her eye targets?** (seen within 0.7 s at 10 m, within 1.5 s at 15 m off-centre, never crouched in the dim) | **Keep them.** Her eyes do what you asked. Tune what she does after she sees you, so the long sight feeds planning (Cut, Ambush) instead of a catch |
| **Q3** | **Should a touch outside a Hunt still kill?** | **Owner: yes.** Preserve outside-Hunt touch lethality (including the existing Lure exception); do not implement L1 |
| **Q4** | **May her sprint drop below 7.5 m/s?** | **Owner: no.** Keep the sprint at 7.5 m/s; exclude L3 and the 7.0 m/s test arms |
| **Q5** | **Should the bots see her spotlight?** | **Yes.** A person sees it, so the bots should, or the tests measure a harder game than players get |

## 4. The levers

Only the allowed levers become settings. L1 and L3 below are historical proposals rejected on
12 November and must not be built or included in runs. The defaults change only when you choose
(task T3.1).

| # | Lever | What it changes | Cause it answers | The cost |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **L1 — rejected** | **Only a Hunt or an ambush kills** (Q3) | A touch during Roam, Check, Search or Back off starts a Hunt instead of ending the shift | 3 (39% of catches) | Bumping into her stops being certain death. With L2 you get a step's head start; without it, the Hunt starts at arm's length |
| **L2** | **A reaction time before she sprints** | A Hunt starts at a hurry and reaches her sprint after 0.75 s or 1.5 s | 2 | It gives back some of the head start the old warning gave (1 s, about 6.5 m), but silently, with no cue: you see her turn, you don't hear a warning |
| **L3 — rejected** | **A slower sprint** (Q4) | 7.5 → 7.0 m/s: she gains 0.5 m a second instead of 1 | 2 | The marketing fact "15% faster in a chase" changes (the second builder's `facts.json`) |
| **L4** | **A Hunt only starts close** | Beyond 20 m she checks, cuts or lies in wait instead of sprinting | 1, 2 | From far off, she plans instead of racing, which is what the long sight was for. A far-off sighting stops being an instant chase |
| **L5** | **Check walks** | Check moves at her walk (3.4 m/s), not a hurry (4.2) | 3 | She checks a sighting more slowly, so it's staler when she gets there |
| **L6** | **More quiet at the start** | The quiet start, when no Hunt may begin, doubles: from 30 s to 60 s in a 150 s test shift, and from 60 s to 120 s in the game's 5-minute shift. The Hunt cooldown goes from 90 s to 120 s | 1, 2 | Fewer, later hunts |
| **L7** | **The bots see her spotlight** (Q5) | A bot that sees her lit patch of floor, in view and with a clear line, treats it like seeing her nearby | 4 | Not a change to her: it makes the test match a person. It may lower catches on its own, so it goes in first and is measured on its own |

**Not levers here:** her eyes (Q2), the blink dash, Throw and Cut, which aren't causing the
catches.

**One new term:**
- **A parameter sweep** is trying a handful of combinations of settings and measuring each one the
  same way.
- **In Kehai,** each combination of levers is one short batch run of bot shifts on the same seeds,
  and the results go in one table.
- **Real-world example:** an online shop showing different prices to different visitors to see
  which sells best (an A/B test), or a recipe tried at a few oven temperatures.

## 5. How it's tested

**Fixed before any run:**

| | Metric | Passes when |
|---|---|---|
| **Primary 1** | Catches per shift, training players, rung C | inside Q1's band (proposed 0.20–0.35) |
| **Primary 2** | Shifts a bot finishes, training players | at least 30% for the efficient player |
| **Primary 3** | Fairness violations | 0 |
| **Primary 4** | Losses measured: times she loses sight of you during a Hunt | at least 30 in a run, so the gap after a loss can finally be measured and compared |

**Secondary:** what she was doing at each catch, first detection, catches you never saw coming
(count and share), Hunts per shift, how far away she was when each Hunt started, seconds from
first sight to moving at you, Cut and Throw counts, combos found, seconds outside her area (must
stay 0), and setpoint error compared only between shifts of similar length.

**The rules:**
- **The cautious player is never used to choose.** The sweep runs only the efficient, skittish
  and reckless players. The cautious player is run once, after you've chosen, and reported on its
  own.
- **Paired by seed,** with standard errors. The same seed gives the same start, not an identical
  shift (`KAREN_RESULTS.md`), and the standard errors include that.
- **Bots aren't people.** The sweep picks a starting point; your playtest (Y5.1) decides.
- **Negative results are reported**, including a lever that does nothing.

**The runs:**

| Run | What | Shifts | About |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The sweep** (T2.1) | 6 allowed combinations (below), rung C, training players, 2 careers, shifts 3–8 | 6 × 72 | re-estimate before the owner-started run; editor closed |
| **The check** (T3.2) | your chosen combination against today's 0.2.0: rungs C and E, all four players, 3 careers, shifts 3–8 | 2 × 144 | 1–2 h, editor closed |

**The 6 allowed combinations (revised before any run):**

| # | Levers | Asks |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | none | today's Karen under the chosen Q5 setting |
| 1 | L2 at 0.75 s | a short reaction time |
| 2 | L2 at 1.5 s | a longer reaction time |
| 3 | L4 | start hunts only when close |
| 4 | L5, L6 | walking Check and more quiet |
| 5 | L2 at 0.75 s, L4, L5, L6 | the allowed changes together |

Fix Q5 before this sweep; use the same setting in every arm. If approved, measure L7 on
its own on paired current-build seeds first. Do not rely on an older build's ablation for
that comparison. Neither outside-Hunt lethality nor sprint speed changes in any arm.
