# Karen does her tricks at the thing (plan, your calls of 25 November)

**Draft for your yes.** Nothing is built yet: Karen's changes are planned first, then built as
one PR.

## The rule

For every trick that uses a thing in the store, Karen:

1. **walks to the thing,**
2. **stands in front of it, facing it, for 3 seconds,** with today's warning sound starting as she
   arrives and the thing showing what she's doing,
3. **then the trick happens.**

**You can stop her:** if she sees you within about 4 m during those 3 seconds, she gives the trick
up and comes for you instead. No chore is left behind.

Example, The overtime: she walks into the staff room, stops at the time clock, its display
flickers while the punch buzz plays, and after 3 s the time stops and blinks red. If you walk in
on her in those 3 s, the clock keeps running and she turns on you.
